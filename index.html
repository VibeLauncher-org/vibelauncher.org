Features

What it does

  • Instances

    Several game directories, each with its own version, loader and mods.

  • Versions and loaders

    Vanilla, Forge, Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge.

  • Mods and modpacks

    Modrinth and CurseForge. Six imported formats.

  • Microsoft sign-in

    Your own account. The tokens stay in the system keyring.

  • Java

    Detected on the machine, downloaded from Adoptium when missing.

  • Servers

    The servers of an instance, pinged and joined from the launcher.

  • Vibefork

    Adapt a mod or a modpack with your own AI model.

  • Diagnostics

    Share a game log on mclo.gs, only after you confirm it.

Every file of a modpack is checked by its hash before it is installed. What the launcher sends — and what it does not.

Vibefork

Adapt a mod or a modpack with your own model

Vibefork changes a mod or a modpack with an AI model of your choice. You decide what to keep. The model is yours, and the changes are made on your machine.

FAQ

Questions

Does it need Java?

Java is detected on your machine; the launcher can download a runtime from Adoptium when it is missing.

Does it phone home?

No. The project runs no servers of its own, and the launcher has no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Where are my tokens?

In the keyring of your operating system, never in a configuration file or a log.

How do I import a modpack?

Six formats are read: Modrinth, CurseForge, ATLauncher, FTB, Technic and packwiz. Every file is checked by its hash.

Is it signed? Will my antivirus complain?

The builds are not signed with a code signing certificate, so SmartScreen may ask once on Windows. On macOS the app is signed ad hoc and not notarized.

How do I report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub, or write to vibelauncher@vibefork.me.
